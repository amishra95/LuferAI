# Surface brief: public site and app world

Scope: the landing page (Persuade) leads; sign-in/sign-up, legal pages (Read) and every dashboard (Operate) inherit the same world through shared tokens, fonts and components.
Audience: the company organiser in Bengaluru; finance and approvers second; venues on the supply side.
Action: "Plan your first event" (sign-up), "List your venue" second.
Proof on hand: the real product flow (brief, matched venues, GST split, approvals) as illustrative UI; 29 real Bengaluru listings; venue photography in public/landing. No customer names, quotes or numbers until the user supplies them.
Stamp ink: on the landing and auth pages every stamp is violet ink, told apart by its word alone. In the app, status stamps keep semantic ink (green done, yellow pending, red refused, carbon neutral) because people scan long tables for state; the word always leads and colour only confirms it.
Sample data: every illustrative sheet (hero invoice, matched-venues preview) is marked SPECIMEN.
Unresolved: the ITC example on a restaurant dinner awaits a CA's check (section 17(5)(b)).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lufer is the bill book every finance team already trusts. An event is written up once as line items, stamped as it passes each check, with copies for everyone. Refuses the category default: a split hero of headline beside a dashboard screenshot over rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon-blue ink (#2b3a8c) on white original sheets; pink duplicate and yellow triplicate copies as secondary surfaces; pale blue ruling and double rules; violet rubber stamps (IN POLICY, APPROVED, PAID) as the state language, outlined and slightly rotated on the landing, upright in the app. Anek Latin (Ek Type, Indian foundry) for every voice, wide for headers; Courier Prime only for written-in entries and amounts on sheets.

STORY: The visitor sees their own event written up as a tax invoice, sees it stamped in policy and approved, believes the books will be right, and starts planning.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Carbon-blue ground. Left: headline in wide white Anek, one line of support, a yellow button "Plan your first event". Right: an open bill book page, No. 0419, line items in carbon blue type, the pink duplicate peeking beneath, two violet stamps landing on it.

FORM: GST Bill Book (Indian duplicate/triplicate bill books), my top-ranked grounded candidate, chosen by the user over the roll. seed:ca3c826db15440a6. Signature interaction: the stamps land in sequence on the hero sheet (IN POLICY, then APPROVED) once on load, settled immediately under reduced motion. Cross-surface reach: dashboard cards are white sheets with a ruled header; statuses are stamps; the sidebar is the bill book's carbon-blue cover.
